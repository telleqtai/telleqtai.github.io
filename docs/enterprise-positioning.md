# Enterprise positioning and research

Updated: 2026-09-06.

## Positioning

Audience: enterprises already running on SAP and other interconnected legacy systems, not only ERP platforms. Their core systems are essential to operations, but accumulated dependencies, inconsistent data, and manual cross-system processes constrain further modernization and AI adoption.

Telleqt builds an Operational Data Layer alongside those systems. It acts as a second operational heart: synchronizing relevant data, maintaining shared business context and live states, and connecting AI with workflows through agreed interfaces. Existing systems retain their operational role. The first engagement establishes the layer for one valuable use case, rather than promising enterprise-wide replacement or instant modernization.

Lead the website with reduced operational losses without a prerequisite core-system rebuild: "Cut operational losses with AI. No legacy rebuild." Target large manufacturers and enterprises already dependent on SAP or other legacy systems. Keep first-screen language understandable to executives. The Operational Data Layer explains how we deliver; it is not the central marketing headline. Professional Services are the core engagement. ALEX and NowMem are optional accelerators, used when they improve the solution, not mandatory dependencies or the complete Operational Data Layer.

## First engagement

Offer one priority workflow, real business data, and measurable outcomes. Agree the operational loss, baseline, and success criteria; connect the relevant systems; deliver a working AI workflow; and evaluate the result against implementation and AI running costs. The first month covers a scoped workflow with data readiness, access, and integration approvals confirmed before kickoff. Broader rollout depends on evidence, not a promise of instant enterprise-wide transformation.

The differentiator is the concrete combination of delivery, time to first working results, core-system continuity, and accountable economics. Do not claim exclusive ownership of side-by-side integration or guaranteed savings. A substantiated customer example is still needed to prove performance.

## Three priorities

These are an editorial synthesis selected for Telleqt's stated capabilities and audience. They are not a statistically established ranking of the world's top three enterprise problems. Examples and proposed KPIs below are application hypotheses, not Telleqt case studies or promised results.

### 1. Legacy complexity constrains modernization

SAP describes how extensive customization and redundant integrations can obstruct innovation, and identifies incremental steps available before a cloud move. This supports positioning around dependencies in an existing operational landscape, not failed SAP installation.

Source: [SAP, Accelerate Innovation with a Clean Core Strategy](https://news.sap.com/2024/01/accelerate-innovation-with-clean-core-strategy/), January 22, 2024. This is vendor guidance, not prevalence research.

Telleqt application: establish a decoupled data and intelligence layer with controlled interfaces and phased deployment. Example: introducing production exception management where changing tightly coupled ERP customizations would otherwise delay rollout. Measure deployment lead time and adoption of the selected use case; check operational stability alongside benefits.

### 2. Inconsistent cross-system data prevents dependable AI

IBM's 2025 CEO study surveyed 2,000 CEOs across 33 countries and 24 industries. Half reported that recent investment had left their organizations with disconnected technology; 68% identified integrated enterprise-wide data architecture as critical for cross-functional collaboration. These findings are cross-industry, not SAP-specific.

Source: [IBM CEO study release](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2025-05-06-ibm-study-ceos-double-down-on-ai-while-navigating-enterprise-hurdles), May 6, 2025; fieldwork February-April 2025.

Telleqt application: align definitions and identifiers, reconcile records, and synchronize the operational states needed by the use case. Example: ERP, warehouse, and production applications disagree about available inventory. Measure consistency, completeness, freshness, and downstream decision quality. Synchronization alone does not establish data quality, semantics, or permission to use data.

### 3. Manual exceptions create operational value loss

The 2026 Celonis Process Optimization Report found that 76% of respondents said current processes held back their agentic-enterprise ambitions; 58% of process and operations leaders reported departmental silos. Insight Avenue conducted 1,649 interviews in June-July 2025 at organizations with revenue above $500 million. Celonis commissioned the research, so it supplies relevant directional evidence rather than a neutral global ranking.

Source: [Celonis, The Enterprise AI Reality Check](https://www.celonis.com/news/press/the-enterprise-ai-reality-check-high-ambitions-meet-operational-barriers), February 4, 2026.

McKinsey's March 2025 survey analysis associated workflow redesign with self-reported EBIT impact from generative AI. This is correlation, not proof of causal savings or Telleqt performance.

Source: [McKinsey, How organizations are rewiring to capture value](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-how-organizations-are-rewiring-to-capture-value), March 12, 2025.

Telleqt application: automate cross-system exception detection, prioritization, and handling with human review where needed. Examples: purchasing and fulfillment discrepancies, production replanning, or inventory imbalances. Measure rework cost, exception handling effort, working capital, and avoidable downtime according to the selected process.

## Copy boundaries

- SAP already offers Business AI and Joule. Avoid blanket claims that SAP lacks native AI. Availability and practical fit depend on product, version, deployment, integration, and data readiness. [SAP Business AI announcement](https://news.sap.com/2025/05/sap-business-ai-reimagine-how-enterprises-run/), May 20, 2025.
- Say "AI alongside existing SAP and other legacy systems," not "stalled SAP initiative" or language implying that Telleqt installs or rescues ERP implementations.
- "Without rebuilding the legacy core" describes the intended architecture. Do not imply no integration work, no required access, universal compatibility, or guaranteed zero disruption.
- The owner-specified offer is "First working results within a month. Not a year.", prominently stated in the first-engagement section following the hero and supported by the delivery section. This refers to a focused first workflow, not complete enterprise rollout or guaranteed financial returns. The contrast concerns time to first results, not an unverified claim about every competitor. Confirm scope, access, interfaces, and approvals with the client; broader rollout depends on readiness and measured results.
- Keep industry survey numbers separate from client outcomes. Publish Telleqt case-study metrics only with their actual scope and evidence.
- Root HTML files remain the website source of truth; do not regenerate them from Stitch or the ignored build script.
